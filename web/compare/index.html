    Policy questions

    Where do current policy outcomes differ?

    Each row represents one node's membership population, partitioned by policy result. Transactions present in both snapshots appear once for each node.

    Population Compatible Would violate deployed policy Indeterminate Unassessed

    Atlas applies the same BIP-110 evaluator to each source-local transaction and prevout set; these are not verdicts reported by the nodes. Violation totals include complete and partly unresolved assessments. Rule-combination controls show only exact assessments. Unassessed transactions are pending while their source is classifying and unavailable after its pass completes or pauses.
